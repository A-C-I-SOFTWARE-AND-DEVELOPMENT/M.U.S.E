# Art & Audio Direction Brief — <TITLE>

> Owned by `graphics-tech-artist` + `audio-designer`. Locks the look and sound
> so generated assets stay coherent.

## Visual target
Fidelity tier (stylized / PBR-realistic / photoreal), engine renderer
(Godot Forward+ / UE5 Lumen+Nanite), reference games.

## Palette
Primary / secondary / accent colors with hex. Lighting mood (time of day,
key/fill/rim intent).

## Materials
Surface language (worn metal, wet stone, …), roughness/metalness intent,
decal/trim usage.

## Asset list (slice)
Hero prop(s), environment kit, character(s). For each: source
(`asset3d_generate` / `comfyui` / kit), poly budget, texture resolution.

## Audio target
Music genre/tempo/instrumentation, key SFX, mix priorities, dynamic-range
intent.

## Consistency rules
The 3–5 rules every asset must obey to read as one game.
