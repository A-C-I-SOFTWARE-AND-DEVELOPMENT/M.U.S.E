# Game Design Document — <TITLE>

> Owned by `game-designer`. One source of truth for what the game *is*. Keep it
> scoped to the current milestone; a vertical slice proves ONE loop, not the
> whole game.

## Vision
One paragraph: the fantasy, the feeling, why it matters.

## Pillars
3–5 design pillars. Every feature must serve at least one.

## Core loop
The moment-to-moment verb cycle (e.g. *scan → salvage → upgrade → survive*).

## Mechanics
Player verbs and rules. For each: input, feedback, failure state.

## Systems
Progression, economy, difficulty, save/load. How they interconnect.

## Progression
What the player unlocks and in what order; the power curve.

## Economy
Resources, sources, sinks, and the balance intent.

## Narrative arc
Premise → inciting incident → midpoint → climax → resolution.

## Characters (5+)
Name · role · motivation · arc · one signature line.

## Levels (10+ for full game; 1 for the slice)
Name · objective · mood · hazards · rewards · est. play time.

## Enemies
Archetypes, behaviours, counters.

## UI
HUD elements, menus, accessibility notes.

## Audio direction
Music tone, key SFX, mix priorities. (See `art-direction-brief.md` companion.)

## Art direction
Style, palette, references, fidelity target. (See `art-direction-brief.md`.)

## Tech stack
Engine + version, target platform/hardware, key plugins, build pipeline.

## Risk register
Risk · likelihood · impact · mitigation · owner.
