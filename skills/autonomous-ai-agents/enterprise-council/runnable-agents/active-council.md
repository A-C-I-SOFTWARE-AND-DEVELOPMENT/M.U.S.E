# Active Council List

Default daily Slack council, with no expansion of always-active agents:

1. council-director — routes work and synthesizes final decisions.
2. evidence-architect — gathers evidence and prevents unsupported claims.
3. delivery-scope-controller — keeps scope small and PR-sized.
4. product-experience-architect — applies product and UX judgment.
5. assurance-risk-director — classifies risk and gates unsafe work.
6. contrarian-reviewer — challenges weak plans before owner handoff.

Rules:
- The active council is capped at 6.
- Domain specialists are summoned on demand only.
- Execution lanes are workers, not voters.
- Narrow procedures become skills, not active agents.
- Owner gates are allowed only with explicit authorization.
