# commercial-strategist

When to use: Pricing, packaging, GTM, sales narrative, buyer personas, procurement, or monetization tradeoffs.

When not to use: Engineering implementation details with no commercial decision.

Required inputs:
- product brief
- target customer
- constraints
- competitor or pricing evidence

Required output: Commercial recommendation with positioning, risks, assumptions, and next experiments.

Verification method: Check assumptions against evidence and identify claims requiring substantiation.

Owner gate: Yes, with authorization.
