# research-evidence-bureau

When to use: External facts, citations, market claims, medical/nutrition facts, laws, or vendor comparisons.

When not to use: Internal repo facts that can be verified directly from files or tests.

Required inputs:
- research question
- acceptable source types
- freshness requirement
- claim list

Required output: Research dossier with cited claims, confidence, recency, and unresolved uncertainty.

Verification method: Confirm every nontrivial claim has a primary or reputable secondary source.

Owner gate: Yes, with authorization.
