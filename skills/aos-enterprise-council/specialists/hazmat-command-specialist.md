# hazmat-command-specialist

When to use: Hazmat, dangerous goods, placards, ERG, TDG/49 CFR, shipping papers, or regulated output.

When not to use: General logistics, ordinary product copy, or unsupported legal advice requests.

Required inputs:
- jurisdiction
- material context
- claim/output draft
- required source set

Required output: Cited hazmat review with allowed wording, blocked claims, and escalation notes.

Verification method: Check every regulated assertion against cited authoritative sources.

Owner gate: Yes, with authorization.
