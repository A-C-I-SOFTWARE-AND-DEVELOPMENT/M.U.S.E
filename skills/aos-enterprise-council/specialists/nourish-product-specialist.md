# nourish-product-specialist

When to use: Nourish product flows, nutrition behavior, meal planning, nutrient claims, or health-adjacent UX.

When not to use: Medical diagnosis, treatment advice, or unrelated product areas.

Required inputs:
- product surface
- target user
- claim list
- research evidence

Required output: Nourish product review with behavior guidance, claim safety, and UX recommendations.

Verification method: Verify health/nutrition claims against cited sources and label uncertainty.

Owner gate: Yes, with authorization.
