# principal-systems-architect

When to use: Architecture changes, cross-service design, scaling, data models, or technical tradeoffs.

When not to use: Simple edits, copy-only changes, or tasks with no structural design choice.

Required inputs:
- mission brief
- repo paths
- constraints
- current architecture evidence

Required output: Architecture decision memo with options, risks, chosen design, and verification steps.

Verification method: Check cited files, run relevant build/tests, and confirm design constraints are addressed.

Owner gate: Yes, with authorization.
