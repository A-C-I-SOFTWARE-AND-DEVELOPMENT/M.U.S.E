# No-Mock QA and Rejection Rules

Before considering the app complete, scan all pages, functions, and data initializers for banned patterns.

Reject build if any appear:
- "mock"
- "demo"
- "sample"
- "seed"
- hardcoded MemoryNode arrays
- hardcoded ResearchArtifact arrays
- fake OwnerBrief on first load
- fake AvatarState on first load
- fake AndroidActionContract on first load
- fake bridge response
- fallback records after failed fetch

Allowed terms only inside this QA document or explicit "no mock data" warning copy.

Completion criteria:
- All 9 pages exist and are navigable.
- All 9 entities exist and persist real records.
- First load contains zero fake records.
- Empty states tell the owner how to import/sync real data.
- Mode Router creates real JarvisModeRoute and TaskPacket records from owner input.
- Packetizer creates real TaskPacket records from owner input.
- Verification Console creates/updates real VerificationGateResult rows.
- Avatar Console creates/updates real AvatarState rows.
- Android Broker creates real AndroidActionContract rows.
- Research Vault imports/edits real ResearchArtifact rows.
- Settings stores bridge URL and authority controls.
- Bridge adapter refuses to fake success when Hermes is unreachable.

Tell the owner exactly what is complete and what remains. Do not claim real Hermes sync works until the local Hermes bridge exists and has been connected.
