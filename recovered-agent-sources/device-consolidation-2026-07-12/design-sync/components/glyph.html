Brand glyph

The muse brand mark: a single white --core circle in the void, wrapped by one thin MATTE spectral ring (cyan → violet, single gap, round caps, rotated −32°). Rendered with the .glyph component — inline SVG over the stacked cool-white radial halos of .glyph::before. White is the hero; the ring stays matte (never glowed or neon).
