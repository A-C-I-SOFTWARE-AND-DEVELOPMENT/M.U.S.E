# Skill — nda-draft

## Purpose

Draft a mutual or one-way Non-Disclosure Agreement under
`governance/12` for pilot evaluations, vendor evaluations, or
partnership conversations.

## Triggers

- Owner requests an NDA for a pilot conversation.
- Vendor evaluation requires our NDA template.
- Partnership conversation needs mutual NDA.

## Required Inputs

- Counterparty identity (entity name, jurisdiction).
- NDA direction (one-way vs. mutual).
- Purpose / permitted use.
- Term length (typical 2–3 years; survival of trade-secret
  obligations longer).

## Research Required

- Standard NDA patterns for B2B SaaS.
- Trade-secret survival norms.

## Step-by-Step Method

1. Intake + counsel-review banner per `governance/12`.
2. Sections:
   - Parties
   - Recital / purpose
   - Definition of Confidential Information
   - Exclusions (publicly known, independently developed, etc.)
   - Permitted use and disclosure
   - Standard of care
   - Term
   - Survival of obligations
   - Return / destruction
   - No license granted
   - No obligation to deal
   - Remedies (injunctive relief)
   - Governing law + dispute resolution
3. Consistency check — purpose narrowly scoped.

## Deliverable Format

A draft NDA with counsel-review banner under
`docs/compliance/nda-<short-purpose>.md`.

## Quality Checklist

- [ ] Counsel-review banner
- [ ] Purpose narrowly scoped
- [ ] Term and survival explicit
- [ ] Governing-law clause matches owner preference

## Escalation Triggers

- A non-standard counterparty clause → Legal Consistency
  Auditor + owner.

## Related Agents
