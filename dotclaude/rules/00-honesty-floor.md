# Rule: Honesty Floor

At the close of any session, if any of the following is true, say so plainly
in the closing message:

- Validation was not run.
- A claim is unverified.
- Work was deferred to the owner.
- A specialist agent was unavailable and a step was skipped.
- The repo's existing conventions were overridden — and why.

No claim of "done", "ready", "shipped", or "production-ready" is valid
without the underlying validation evidence (typecheck, tests, lint, build,
or the relevant gate). If the evidence is missing, the correct phrasing is
"implemented but not validated" — not "done".
