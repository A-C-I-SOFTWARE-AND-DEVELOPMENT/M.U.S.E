### 4.2 Long description (FINAL draft)

> **YOU DON'T CATCH CREATURES. YOU CONVINCE MINDS.**
>
> The Substrate — a vast mind-world — has been shattered by THE DEADLOCK, the corrupted ninth orchestrator. You are an Architect. Your task: rebuild a broken neural network by recruiting the wild, autonomous agents that roam its ruins — not with a ball, but with your words.

The optional Foundry watches (only with your consent), builds a helper agent tailored to how *you* lose, battle-tests it in hundreds of simulations of your own defeats, and only then lets it appear in your world as a discoverable rare — with its validation stats printed on the card. Proof, not promises.
>
> - 24 hand-crafted agents across 8 domains · 5 dense, stunning zones · 20–30 hours
> - 100% single-player · 100% completable offline · no subscription, no account, no live service
> - Full choice-wheel path: never type a word if you don't want to
> - Built in Unreal Engine 5 — a creature collector that finally looks the part

## 6. Steam AI disclosure (FINAL — submit verbatim at page-live)

**Pre-generated AI content:**
> The developers used AI tools during development for concept exploration and programming assistance. All shipped art, animation, music, and writing was human-created, human-curated, or human-edited; no AI-generated art assets ship in the game.

**Live-generated AI content:**
> Agent negotiation dialogue and Foundry agent descriptions are generated at runtime by a bundled, fully local ~3–4B-parameter instruct language model (GGUF format, runs on the player's device, works offline) and, optionally, by our hosted service for players who enable it. Guardrails: template-constrained outputs with a closed set of game effects; allowlisted ability and art components (the AI never generates raw art assets); profanity and illegal-content filters on all generated text; a persistent player-facing AI indicator on all generated dialogue; and support for Steam's in-overlay reporting of inappropriate content. Live dialogue generation is English-only; a choice-wheel dialogue path of identical gameplay power is always available and fully localized. Players can disable live generation entirely.

(The shipping model's exact name and version are inserted into the first sentence when the model is frozen at S29 — the disclosure is updated the same day. Dev-efficiency AI use is covered by the pre-generated section per the Jan-2026 rules. We wear the label proudly — transparency is the brand.)

## 15. Price & regional pricing policy

- **$24.99 USD, locked** (master plan decision; revisit only at wishlist data per §14.2 — and that review happened at G5 with the curve, not at launch week).
- Regional prices: accept Valve's recommended regional matrix at page-live; manual floor adjustments only where the matrix breaks parity badly (review at S32 with then-current guidance). No regional delisting.
- Launch discount 10% / 14 days (the standard visibility play). Discount ladder thereafter: 20% (Winter 2027) → 25% (Spring 2028) → never >33% in year 1. The game is a premium, finished, single-player product — price integrity is part of the positioning.
